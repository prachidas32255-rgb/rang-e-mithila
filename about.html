About the Artist

Rang-e-Mithila

Rang-e-Mithila is a small effort to bring the beautiful tradition of Mithila art to the world.

Mithila painting is a traditional art form from the Mithila region of Bihar. It is known for its vibrant colours, intricate patterns and stories inspired by nature, mythology and everyday life.

Through Rang-e-Mithila, I want to preserve this beautiful cultural heritage and share the stories, colours and emotions of Mithila through handmade paintings.

Every painting is created with love, patience and a connection to the rich culture of Mithila.
